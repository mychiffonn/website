I built an AI-powered dashboard on Hex showing safety scores for 41 neighborhoods in San Francisco.

The dashboard streams live civic data from DataSF through ClickHouse, combines neighborhood-level indicators into interpretable safety scores, and presents the results as an interactive analytics surface.
