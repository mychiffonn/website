I research **current and future AI that are safe and empowering for more people**. Towards this end, I'm currently interested in the following problems:

- making AI go well for all
- developing AI for low-resource languages and cultures
- designing cooperative AI for other AI agents, and for humans
- AI for good, focusing on learning sciences, climate science, and knowledge work

My technical work focuses on data, simulation, and [evaluation](https://aimslab.stanford.edu/textbook/), to make grounded, predictive, specific claims about AI capability and safety, then improve them.

I'm a [SPAR](https://sparai.org/) mentee (Fall '26) with Dr [Linh Le](https://aigi.ox.ac.uk/people/linh-le/) and [David Williams-King](https://www.linkedin.com/in/david-williams-king/), also affiliated with [Lida Safety](https://www.lidasafety.org/about) and [SEACrowd](https://seacrowd.org/). I also contribute to community research, including Cohere Labs Community (multi-cultural reasoning benchmark), and Apart Research ([AI control in multilingual multi-agent systems](https://github.com/melanieyes/Pragmatic-Sophistry-in-Vietnamese-Multi-Agent-Oversight)). Recently I worked on [SEATauBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28715) (EMNLP Findings '26), and [CoT monitoring behavior](/projects/2025-cot-monitoring).
