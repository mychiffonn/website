I research **current and future AI that are safe and empowering for more people**. I'm interested in technical AI safety, pluralistic AI and agents, currently concerned about:

1. auditing AI and building tools for AI governance ([GPS-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03553), IncidentBench)
2. loss of control risks and scalable oversight ([CoT monitoring](/projects/2025-cot-monitoring))
3. dynamics and safety of multi-agent systems (e.g., collusion, secret loyalties)
4. multilinguality ([SEATauBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28715)), multicultural awareness ([CulturalRiddles](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29968)), and pluralistic alignment
5. AI in epistemic contexts (e.g., persuasion, spread of misinformation)

I'm a [SPAR](https://sparai.org/) Fellow (Fall '26), mentored by Dr. [Linh Le](https://aigi.ox.ac.uk/people/linh-le/) and [David Williams-King](https://www.linkedin.com/in/david-williams-king/) ([Lida Safety](https://www.lidasafety.org/about)),
researching AI policy simulation.
