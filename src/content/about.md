I research **current and future AI that are safe and empowering for more people**. Towards this end, I'm currently interested in the following problems:

- AI safety: tackling governance ([GPS-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03553), IncidentBench), loss of control risks, human disempowerment, and misinformation and disinformation
- pluralistic AI [SEATauBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28715) (multilingual agent), CulturalRiddles (cultural reasoning)
- more [cooperative](https://www.cooperativeai.com/), human-understanding AI agents

My technical work focuses on data, simulation, and [evaluation](https://aimslab.stanford.edu/textbook/), to make grounded, predictive, specific claims about AI capability and safety, then improve them.

I'm a [SPAR](https://sparai.org/) Fellow (Fall '26), mentored by Dr. [Linh Le](https://aigi.ox.ac.uk/people/linh-le/) and [David Williams-King](https://www.linkedin.com/in/david-williams-king/) ([Lida Safety](https://www.lidasafety.org/about)),
researching AI policy simulation and more broadly, technical AI governance.
I also contract for Lida working on multi-agent safety, and for [Equistamp](https://www.equistamp.com/) on inspect-evals.

Previously I worked with [Dr. Samuel Cahyawijaya](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=w5w_WZEAAAAJ&hl=en) (Cohere Labs) through [SEACrowd Apprenticeship](https://seacrowd.org/apprenticeship),
